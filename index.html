THERMAL INTELLIGENCE / 01

A system that knows when it is getting hot.

PACKAGE STACK

  • 6 vertically stacked Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W nodes emulate a dense package stack.
  • Limited exposed surface area restricts heat dissipation to ambient air, creating position-dependent temperatures.
  • Live SoC temperature, CPU frequency, and power expose each node’s thermal-energy state.

MIXTURE OF EXPERTS

  • Each Pi reports its thermal, compute, power, and workload state.
  • Specialized experts propose actions while the gating network determines their relative contributions.
  • The contributions are combined into a tile assignment and returned to the Pi.

ROBOTIC INFRASTRUCTURE MONITORING / 02

From mobile robots to a shared view of structural condition.

MOBILE ROBOT

  • Compact bristle-bots and mobile robots carry cameras and wireless sensors to parts of a structure that are difficult to inspect directly.
  • Small, low-cost platforms make distributed structural monitoring possible without permanently instrumenting every location.
  • Each robot turns a local, difficult-to-reach surface into an observable part of the monitoring system.

CRACK INSPECTION

  • Three robots photograph overlapping regions of the same wall from coordinated positions.
  • Sobel edge detection extracts likely crack boundaries from each camera image.
  • Image stitching combines the separate observations into one wall-scale inspection map.

AR INTERFACE

  • Augmented reality brings robot positions, camera imagery, and detected cracks into the operator’s view.
  • The interface supports robot steering, team coordination, and interpretation of inspection results.
  • Spatially registered information connects physical infrastructure to monitoring evidence and maintenance decisions.

ACOUSTIC IMAGING / 03

Reconstructing buried pipes with sound.

PROBING PLATFORM

  • The Acoustic Probing Lab (APL) is a mobile sensing platform for imaging infrastructure hidden beneath the ground.
  • An ultrasonic transducer couples sound into the surface while an onboard accelerometer records the returning vibration.
  • Position-tagged measurements turn a physical scan into data for subsurface reconstruction.

FIXED-INTERVAL SCAN

  • APL advances by a fixed distance, stops, and repeats the same pulse–echo measurement.
  • Each position contributes one spatial sample of the subsurface acoustic response.
  • The samples assemble into an energy map; the strongest response identifies the buried pipe.

3D RECONSTRUCTION

  • APL repeats the seven-position scan across four parallel survey rows.
  • Each row becomes an acoustic energy slice whose central response locates the pipe cross-section.
  • Interpolating the response centers reconstructs the buried pipe as a continuous three-dimensional geometry.

LOCALLINK / 01

Software, sensors, and light on one local network.

SYSTEM

  • LocalLink keeps device discovery and control inside the local network, with the Windows app as the operator surface.
  • The ESP32 advertises its display and light-sensor capabilities, then receives time, weather, content, and timer commands over UDP.
  • When the app is absent, NTP-backed local time keeps the lightboard working as a standalone clock.

CONTROL APP

  • Weather, memo, and Lightboard controls live in one dark Windows dashboard.
  • The display can stay fixed or rotate through time and weather at a chosen interval.
  • Countdown presets and precise time fields send a temporary timer to the same hardware surface.

AMBIENT DISPLAY

  • Four 8 × 8 WS2812 panels form a 32 × 8 display for clock, weather, and countdown views.
  • A TSL2591 sensor filters ambient-light readings and eases the LEDs toward a comfortable brightness.
  • The clock follows a twelve-month color palette, carrying the calendar’s visual rhythm into a physical object.

LIVE CALENDAR

  • The companion Wallpaper Engine calendar established the twelve-color month palette later carried into the lightboard.
  • The embedded month view retains month switching, adjacent-date context, date selection, and today highlighting.
  • This public version is self-contained and never loads or exposes the original Memo data.
